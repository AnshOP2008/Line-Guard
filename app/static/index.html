    MAINTENANCE PLANNING ASSUMPTION

    How soon would you inspect?

    EDITABLE HORIZON

    Measured once per official test engine at its final observed cycle. Positive means actual remaining life ≤ selected horizon; alert means predicted remaining life ≤ horizon. Changing the horizon explores the fixed test results, without retraining or tuning the model.

    MODEL SELECTION CONTRACT

    Unseen engines. Real evaluation.

    GROUP-SEPARATED

    5-fold engine-grouped cross-validation on the 100 official training engines, 5 random truncation points per held-out engine (500 evaluations). Lowest CV MAE wins, then refit on all 100 and evaluated once on the official 100-engine test set. Engine identifiers are local to each file, not shared physical engines.

    The data contract

    CHRONOLOGICAL

    • Only eight sensor values enter model fitting.
    • Timestamp and anomaly/changepoint labels are excluded.
    • Preprocessing and residual scales fitted on train only.
    • Threshold: 99th percentile of independent normal calibration scores.
    • Same physical rig; generalization to another site is unproven.

    Sensor-fault stress tests

    SYNTHETIC INJECTIONS

    Current spikes, drift and flatlines injected into held-out normal samples. The heuristic flags exact flatlines and unusually large jumps. It may miss gradual drift and confuse process changes with sensor issues.

    If a signal disappears

    SENSOR ABLATION

    Each sensor is replaced by its training mean, with no model refit. This measures sensitivity to missing information; it is not a recommendation to impute silently in production.
